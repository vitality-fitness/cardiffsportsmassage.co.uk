I treat neck and shoulder tension at [The Health and Wellness Hub](/about/) on Merthyr Road in North Cardiff. Most of this work is for people who sit at a desk and feel the tightness build through the week. A lot of people also hold stress in their neck and shoulders.

The muscles I find tight most often are the upper trapezius, across the top of the shoulder, and levator scapulae, at the side and back of the neck.

## Headaches that start in the neck

I work with headaches that start at the base of the skull and move around toward the temple. They often sit alongside a stiff neck and tight shoulders.

See a GP first if the headache does not start in the neck, came on suddenly and severely, follows a blow to the head, or comes with dizziness, visual change, numbness, weakness, or feeling unwell.

## What happens in the session

I usually start with you lying face down, then ask you to sit up. I choose the techniques to suit you on the day. I often use soft tissue release, muscle energy technique, trigger point therapy and cupping. I will usually ask about the desk you use at work and at home: which desk and chair, and whether you are able to stand.

I usually suggest 45 or 60 minutes for a first visit, which gives time to assess the area and treat it. If a follow-up has settled to one muscle group, a shorter session can be enough. People often feel a change within one or two sessions. At the end, I will suggest what length to book next and how long to leave between visits.

I usually send you away with stretches and strengthening that fit what I found. If the tightness is a recent pull rather than desk tension or stress, wait until any redness has settled and read about [massage after a muscle strain](/muscle-strain-recovery/).
