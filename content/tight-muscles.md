You do not need to be injured, or an athlete, to book. I see anyone who just feels tight, at [The Health and Wellness Hub](/about/) on Merthyr Road in North Cardiff.

The aim is to help you feel looser and move more easily. I will not promise that regular massage stops injuries. At the end of the session I will tell you whether another visit is worth booking, and when. That gap depends on you. I choose the work to suit you on the day. I often use trigger point therapy.

## How long to book

It depends on the muscle group. One area can suit a shorter session. The back usually needs more than 30 minutes, so I usually suggest 45 or 60 minutes for that. If the tightness is painful, or you are not sure what is going on, a first visit of 45 or 60 minutes gives time to assess it.

If you have just pulled a muscle, wait until it is no longer red. That wait, often about five days, is explained in [after a muscle strain](/muscle-strain-recovery/). Desk-related neck and shoulder tension is covered in [neck and shoulder massage](/neck-and-shoulder-massage/), and training tightness in [sports massage for runners](/sports-massage-for-runners/).

I will usually send you away with stretches, and with strengthening where it will help.
