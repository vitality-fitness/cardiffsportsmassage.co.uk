I see runners at [The Health and Wellness Hub](/about/) in North Cardiff, whether you are training for a race or running a few times a week.

What needs work depends on the running. Fell and mountain runners most often come in with tight quads. Road runners more often need hamstrings and calves. I choose the work to suit you on the day. I often use trigger point therapy.

## When to book

A few days after a hard session is often a better time than the day after, when the legs are too sore to treat usefully. The week before a race is often a good time for a visit.

If you have just pulled a muscle, I will usually wait until the redness has settled. That is often about five days, and it is described on the [muscle strain](/muscle-strain-recovery/) page. I do little foot work. Foot pain is usually better seen by a podiatrist.

## What to book

I usually suggest 45 or 60 minutes for a first visit about a real niggle. A follow-up on one muscle group can be shorter. The back usually needs more than 30 minutes. Most people feel a change within one or two sessions. At the end, I will suggest what length to book next and how long to leave between visits.

If you are not injured and the legs just feel tight, [tight muscles](/tight-muscles/) explains that visit. Treatment can help you feel looser and move more easily. I will not promise that you will stay free of injury. I will also usually tell you when you can train again and how to resume.
