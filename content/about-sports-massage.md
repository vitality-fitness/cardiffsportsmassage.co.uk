We provide a friendly and professional deep-tissue sports massage service in Cardiff.  We are consistently highly-rated by our many [satisfied customers](/reviews).

For almost 20 years, we have delivered a wide variety of treatments ranging from major sports injuries to work-related neck and shoulder aches.

We can provide treatment and advice for many conditions, including:

- [Neck and shoulder tension](/neck-and-shoulder-massage/) from desk work, including some headaches
- [Tight or tired muscles](/tight-muscles/) when nothing is actually injured
- A [muscle strain](/muscle-strain-recovery/) once the redness has settled
- Before, during and after special events
- Injury prevention, recovery and rehabilitation
- Stretching and flexibility advice

We also see [runners](/sports-massage-for-runners/) on the road or on the fells.

## Sports massage in North Cardiff

The clinic is at [The Health and Wellness Hub](/about/) on Merthyr Road (CF14 1DL), with free off-street parking. Donna is a Level 4 sports massage therapist and the sole therapist at Vitality Fitness, so you always see the same person.

Whether you are managing neck and shoulder tension from desk work, recovering from a running injury, or rehabilitating after a sports injury, Donna can tailor treatment to you.
