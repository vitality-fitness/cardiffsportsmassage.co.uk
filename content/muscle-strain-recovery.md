I can see a pulled muscle once the inflammation has gone down. I look for redness, which has often settled after about five days. If the area is still red, I will ask you to wait.

I treat most strains. I do little foot work, and will usually suggest a podiatrist for a foot problem.

## What the session is for

A strained muscle still needs time. Once the area has settled, I usually use the visit to increase blood flow, reduce waste products, and work on the muscle fibres. I also want the muscle moving through a comfortable range again. I choose the techniques to suit you on the day. I often use trigger point therapy.

You leave with a way to use it, suited to how it feels. A runner who had pulled a calf, for example, might be asked to start with a shorter run at about 60% effort. I will usually also suggest stretches and strengthening, and tell you when training can resume.

## How long to book

I usually suggest 45 or 60 minutes for the first visit. A later visit can be shorter when only one muscle group needs work. The back usually needs more than 30 minutes. People often notice a change within one or two sessions. I will suggest the next length, and the gap between appointments, at the end of the visit.

Ongoing desk tension is covered under [neck and shoulder massage](/neck-and-shoulder-massage/). Training tightness that is not a fresh strain is covered under [sports massage for runners](/sports-massage-for-runners/).
